The story

Eleven years of work that someone would rather didn't exist.

Dr Jarrad Branthwaite and Chris Wood spent eleven years on work that certain people would rather didn't exist. They didn't put it in a file. They hid it in this phone, behind a chain of locks.

Every lock gives up a number. Find them all, in order, and they open the message he left behind. Then wait for Chris.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Unsettled really impossible?

No, but it is hard. Every level is tested to have exactly one answer, and everything you need is already in front of you.

Is there a walkthrough?

No. That's the point. Earn a hint every five levels, or buy a hint pack if you're truly stuck.

Is it suitable for escape room fans?

Yes. Unsettled is a digital escape room where the room is a phone: ciphers, hidden clues and misdirection, with a spy story told through sealed messages.

How much does it cost?

Chapters one and two are free. Chapter three is a one-off £2.99 unlock, and three extra hints cost £0.99. There are no ads.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
